GTA6 Shorts — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

GTA6 Shorts is a private automation tool operated by a single individual (the "Owner"). It is used only to prepare short gaming videos and publish them to the Owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account. The tool has no other users and is not offered to the public.

What data the tool accesses

  • YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API: the Owner's own channel information, videos and analytics, and public search results for gaming videos. Used to find source material, upload the Owner's videos, schedule their publication and measure their performance.
  • Instagram Graph API: the Owner's own Instagram professional account, its media and insights, and public information of other business/creator accounts (Business Discovery) for content research.
  • No data of any other YouTube or Instagram user is collected beyond publicly available video and account statistics.

How data is stored

  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted in the Owner's n8n Cloud workspace and are never shared.
  • Video files are stored temporarily (up to 2 days) in the Owner's private cloud storage and on the Owner's computer, then deleted.
  • Public statistics (views, likes, titles) are stored in the Owner's n8n workspace for analysis.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with, or disclosed to third parties. Data is processed only by the services the tool runs on (n8n Cloud, Supabase, OpenRouter for text generation, Telegram for notifications to the Owner).

YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

Deleting data

The Owner can revoke access in the Google and Meta account settings at any time; all stored tokens then stop working. Stored statistics are deleted on request to the contact below.

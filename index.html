GTA6 Shorts

GTA6 Shorts is a private automation tool that prepares short gaming videos about GTA 6 and publishes them to its owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account. It has a single user (the owner) and is not offered to the public.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

What it does

  • Finds recent public GTA 6 videos on YouTube (YouTube Data API) and credits their authors.
  • Uploads the owner's own edited Shorts to the owner's channel and schedules them.
  • Reads the owner's channel statistics (YouTube Analytics API) to plan content.
